Projects

What Drove Kenya’s Inflation in 2026?

Python, pandas, pdfplumber, matplotlib

Extracted and cleaned 140 rows of data from ten monthly KNBS Consumer Price Index reports to find which spending categories drove inflation. Inflation rose from 4.4% to 7.3%, and food and transport accounted for roughly 5 of the 6.6 points explained by the 13 categories. The write-up also explains why fixed basket weights understate fast-rising categories.

Kenya Credit Risk by Sector

Python, pdfplumber, Power BI

Analysed non-performing loans across 11 sectors using the Central Bank of Kenya’s Bank Supervision Annual Report 2025. Banks held Ksh 4,347 billion in loans, 16.0% of them non-performing. Building and Construction had the highest default rate (29.5%), while Personal and Household was the largest sector but among the lowest in default rate (9.7%). Includes a two-chart Power BI dashboard.
